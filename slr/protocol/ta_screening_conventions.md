# Screening conventions (final, 2026-09-06) — PRISMA 2020 title/abstract stage

Review: AI integration in supply chain digital twins, human-in-the-loop (HITL) focus; SCOR used for coding. Search Scopus + WoS 2010–2026.

INCLUSION requires all three, evidenced in title, abstract or keywords (never inferred):
1. Digital twin or digital model (of an operational system, asset or process).
2. Supply chain or logistics setting, including intra-plant logistics and supply chain planning.
3. AI or machine learning.

CODES: I include; E1 no twin/model; E2 no supply chain/logistics framing (also out-of-scope domains such as clinical patient twins); E3 no AI/ML; E4 review/survey/overview/bibliometric/editorial/literature-based framework (kept for backward snowballing with a value alto/medio/bajo); E5 not in English / not peer-reviewed / non-screenable publication type; E6 undetected duplicate. Exclusion reason column maps: E1 "No digital twin/model", E2 "No supply chain scope", E3 "No AI/ML component", E4 "Wrong publication type", E5 "Not in English"/"Not peer-reviewed"/"Wrong publication type", E6 "Duplicate".

- C1 threshold: a criterion counts only when the twin, the setting or the AI method is an object, component or method of the study as stated. A passing mention in a list of enablers, a benefit, a trend, future work, an author/index keyword alone, or a title alone (R0613, R0368) does not count. Twin status and implementation are never inferred (R0161, R0227). Exceptions: twin declared as the objective in the title AND keywords with the work as its precursor (R0272, R0551); authors state they modelled the environment and validated it as a replica of the real system (R0306). A twin named only as a future step/structure is E1 (R0285, R0340, R0672).
- C2 primary code = first failing criterion in the order E1 → E2 → E3; secondary failures noted. C6 categories force E1 even when the domain also fails (R0372, R0689).
- C3 reviews: any review, survey, overview, perspective, bibliometric or scientometric study, case synthesis, literature-based conceptual framework, editorial or preface, or Document type = Review, is E4 regardless of topic, with a snowballing value; C3 outranks a C1 failure (R0524, R0597, R0641, R0699, R0678). BUT conceptual papers whose object is the digital twin itself with AI as a constitutive element (Ivanov line) are INCLUDED (R0011, R0084, R0137, R0273, R0309, R0554, R0614, R0664, R0389). A summary of industry solutions is not a review (R0634 → E1).
- Snowballing value: alto = review sits in the exact twin + AI + supply chain intersection; medio = declared scope names supply chain/logistics and also twins or AI as objects, or carries a human-machine collaboration axis; bajo = these appear only as listed enablers or the domain is unrelated.
- C4 adoption/perception/prioritisation/maturity/landscape studies (surveys, PLS-SEM, MCDA, interviews, company scans) where twins and AI are constructs: E1 when the twin is only a construct/mention; E3 when the twin is the object but no AI method is applied or proposed (R0019, R0055, R0196, R0227). Documented real deployments with the twin described are included as Empirical case (R0184, R0201, R0525).
- C5 Make rule: production/shop-floor twins need supply chain or intra-plant logistics framing OF THE STUDY ITSELF (material flow, intra-shop logistics, supply chain operations or service levels named in the abstract: R0025, R0050, R0328, R0331, R0335, R0629, R0643 → I with Make). Generic supply chain sentences, closing sentences, or planning production against demand/lead time do not qualify (R0033, R0088, R0170, R0214, R0364, R0384, R0386, R0586, R0595, R0608, R0612, R0615 → E2).
- C6 twin of operations: physics/data generators (radio propagation R0357, synthetic imagery R0662), data/document containers (product passports, NFT records, QR registries), biological models (R0316, R0656), design models (R0365), market constructs (R0128, R0363), copies of persons (R0689, R0372) → E1. Perishable product-state twins in the cold chain are included (R0119, R0302, R0322). Clinical/patient twins → E2 (R0216 and the healthcare group). Healthcare or humanitarian supply-network twins are included (R0521, R0584, R0350).
- C7 agent-based and multi-agent/agentic systems count as AI (R0295, R0520, R0564, R0350).
- C8 (three rulings by the screener of record): (1) AI only mentioned (keyword, adjective such as "intelligent"/"cognitive"/"AI-driven", enabler, capability, future possibility) does not count: R0532, R0511, R0565, R0553, R0566, R0585 → E3. (2) AI used counts even when never labelled AI: named decision/prediction models implemented in the studied system (R0591 scheduling and path-planning models; R0629 two-layer optimisation in the twin control loop) → I. (3) Good examples are kept: an AI capability integrated in a documented real deployment (R0525 Ford SCDT), a predictive analytics tool used to produce the twin's forecasts (R0621 SAP Analytics Cloud), AI constitutive of a conceptual supply chain twin (R0554, R0614, R0664, R0389). Not counted: a tool without a stated predictive/learning function (R0631 DSS, R0686 Cognitive Advisor), the twin's own simulation engine (R0635, R0633, R0566), a method used only as the researchers' analysis aid (R0523 association rules, R0577 surrogate models), AI as a future use of the model (R0373), AI orchestrating compute only (R0238), conceptual possibility only (R0256). Modest implemented methods count (R0299: multivariate regression + A*). Fuzzy optimisation and metaheuristics alone were not treated as AI (R0511) unless agents or a learning method are named (R0520, R0564 ant colony + agent-based simulation).
- Retention pool "Antecedente C8": E3 records with an implemented or concretely designed twin AND a central supply chain/logistics setting keep code E3 and carry "[Antecedente C8: alto|medio]" in Notes (alto = implemented/validated twin, medio = conceptual twin or panorama).
- Cyber-defence: twin + RL whose decisions are defence mechanisms → E2 (R0527); decoy twins generating attack data → E1 (R0606); SCADA/grid twins → E2.
- Teaching/training tools → E2 (R0281, R0539, R0540, R0598); R0695 is E1 (no twin of its own). Vehicle/engine energy twins with logistics only as context → E2 (R0043, R0122, R0590).
- Language: database language discrepancy with a journal in another language → E5 (R0053, R0132, R0344); WoS Language = German → E5 (R0712). One-sentence extended abstract → E5 (R0339).
- Sensitivity at title/abstract: when the twin and the supply chain setting are central and a concrete AI method or component is named in the abstract as part of the proposed system, include and let the full text decide (R0012, R0013, R0076).

## Final audit (2026-09-06) — clarifications applied
- C3 covers every record whose abstract declares a review-type method or genre: literature review/synthesis as the basis of a conceptual framework, perspective or position article, compilation of a research stream, trend analysis with secondary case synthesis, state-of-the-art review followed by a framework, sector overview with company examples, editorial column → E4 with a snowballing value, even in the exact intersection (R0093, R0335 → E4 alto). Theory-building conceptual papers not resting on a declared review whose object is the AI-enabled supply chain twin remain includes (Ivanov line: R0011, R0084, R0137, R0273, R0309, R0554, R0614, R0664). Expository papers with no declared review method and no twin of their own follow C1/C2 (R0207, R0288 → E1). R0389 is no longer in the Ivanov list (AI only enunciated → E3 pool medio).
- C8 label versus articulated role: AI only as a label of the systems described → E3 (pool when twin and setting are central): R0147, R0133, R0234, R0389. Proposed SC twin frameworks with AI constitutive and its role articulated stay I (R0530, R0022); AI used without a named method stays I (R0347, R0201); R0107 (implemented twin, ML a proposed component) stays I per the third ruling.
- Companion reports (pairs): second appearance → E6 "Duplicate", note "informe compañero de R0xxx" (R0155 ← R0152; R0315 ← R0298). Distinct studies by the same group are kept (R0272 vs R0298; R0328 vs R0331, cross-linked).
- C5: R0080 kept as the weakest Make (explicit OSCM framing + direct HITL example; veto possible). R0081, R0341 → E2 (generic supply chain sentence only).
- C6 applied: R0195 (design/assembly), R0548 (garment design), R0673 (optical design), R0594 (NFT data containers) → E1. C4: R0217 → E1.
- Totals after audit: 116 I (23 Make); 596 excluded = E1 147, E2 90, E3 37 (pool 31: alto 18, medio 13), E4 315 (alto 33, medio 90, bajo 192), E5 5, E6 2.
