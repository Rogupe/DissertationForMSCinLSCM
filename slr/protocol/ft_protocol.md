# Full-text eligibility and coding protocol (PRISMA 2020, full-text stage)

Review: how far AI is integrated in supply chain digital twins and what role human-in-the-loop (HITL) involvement plays. Coding framework: SCOR. The screener of record is Rodrigo; you PROPOSE, he confirms. Never invent content: every judgement must be backed by verbatim quotes from the full text with the section where they appear. If the full text is not accessible (paywall, captcha, sign-in wall, PDF-only that you cannot read), report status NOT_RETRIEVED with what you saw and stop; do not code from the abstract.

## Full-text eligibility (all three required)
1. Digital twin or digital model of an operational system, asset or process: implemented, simulated, or concretely designed (architecture with components and data flows); a purely conceptual twin is acceptable only when the paper's object is the twin concept itself (theory-building papers). Twin status is never inferred; a plain simulation model that the authors never call a twin/digital model fails unless they validate it as a replica of a real system.
2. Supply chain or logistics setting, including intra-plant logistics and supply chain planning. A production/shop-floor twin qualifies only if the paper itself frames it in supply chain or logistics terms (material flow, suppliers, deliveries, service level, inventory, intra-plant logistics).
3. AI or machine learning method used in, or constitutively designed into, the twin/system: named method executed or specified (ML/DL models, RL, metaheuristics with learning, multi-agent/agentic systems, LLMs, predictive analytics tools producing the twin's forecasts). AI only as a keyword, adjective, enabler, or future work fails. Reviews/surveys fail (code Other: review).
Exclusion reasons (use exactly): `No AI/ML component`, `No digital twin / digital model`, `No supply chain / logistics scope`, `Not peer-reviewed / low-quality venue`, `Other (note reason)`.

## Coding fields (only for FT_INCLUDE; use the exact vocabularies)
- evidence_type: Conceptual | Review | Simulation/synthetic | Empirical case | Empirical data
- sector: short label (Automotive, Food/cold chain, Warehousing, Maritime/port, Aviation, Retail, Healthcare, Energy, Generic/cross-sector, ...)
- scor_plan / scor_source / scor_make / scor_deliver / scor_return: Y | N (which SCOR processes the twin covers), end_to_end: Y | N (twin spans supplier-to-customer)
- ai_technique: RNN/LSTM/GRU | Other deep learning | Classical ML | Metaheuristic/evolutionary | Reinforcement learning | Hybrid | LLM/agent | Other
- ai_role: Forecasting/estimation | Optimisation | Anomaly detection | Simulation surrogate | Control | Decision support | Other
- integration_extent: 1 Descriptive | 2 Diagnostic | 3 Predictive | 4 Prescriptive-advisory | 5 Prescriptive-autonomous (1 twin mirrors state, AI only representation/monitoring; 2 AI explains deviations/detects anomalies; 3 AI forecasts or corrects parameters, decisions external; 4 AI/optimisation proposes decisions, a human approves or selects; 5 system executes decisions without human approval)
- hitl_role: Elevated | Maintained | Reduced | Replaced | Not discussed (how the human role changes as AI is integrated)
- hitl_function: Approve/override | Set objectives/constraints | Handle exceptions | Provide data/labels | Interpret outputs | None | Not discussed
- hitl_conditions: conditions under which the human role changes (own words, <= 40 words) or "Not discussed"
- key_finding: own words, <= 50 words
- citation_locator: section names/numbers (and page numbers if visible) for the key finding and the HITL evidence
- use_in_typology: Y | N (Y when the paper gives a clear AI-role + HITL configuration usable for the typology)
- quotes: 3 to 6 verbatim quotes (<= 40 words each) with section, covering: the twin, the setting, the AI method, the HITL statement (if any)
- ft_note: <= 60 words, Spanish, why included/excluded, doubts for the screener of record
